# Tokyo Nights Racing Master

## Identity

Tokyo Nights Racing is a Unity/C# arcade racing systems portfolio project.

It demonstrates a connected gameplay architecture rather than claiming to be
a finished commercial racing game.

## Core proof

- ScriptableObject-driven vehicles and race modes;
- garage selection and saved equipped-vehicle state;
- checkpoint-based player and AI race progress;
- custom waypoint racing AI;
- vehicle-class-aware AI selection;
- freeroam traffic and density settings;
- mission-marker race launching;
- persistent settings and audio routing;
- loading, return-state, and scene-flow handling;
- editor tools for repetitive setup work.

## Historical role

Tokyo Nights Racing is the archived game-development proof and testbed.

Future commercial Unity products are separate projects:

- Mission Marker Toolkit;
- Vehicle Setup Wizard;
- Traffic System Lite.

Those products may reuse lessons and selected systems, but they do not reopen
Tokyo Nights Racing as an endless game-polish project.

## Release boundary

The public release is a technical portfolio prototype. It does not claim:

- commercial-game completeness;
- production support;
- ownership of third-party assets;
- a current multiplayer or online service;
- that every future improvement listed in historical notes will be built.

## Completion gate

Tokyo Nights Racing v0.1.0 is complete when:

- Unity 2022.3.62f1 resolves packages from a clean machine-independent
  manifest and imports the project without compiler errors;
- all five enabled build scenes remain present;
- repository and privacy validation passes;
- the eleven-image screenshot group is present, distinct, and approved;
- README, rights, reproduction, security, and release documentation agree;
- GitHub validation is green;
- the release tag points to clean and synchronized main.
